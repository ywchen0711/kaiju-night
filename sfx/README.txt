Kaiju Night sound effects

Built by tools/design_sfx.py from real recordings, all CC0 (public domain):
  Kenney (www.kenney.nl): Impact Sounds  https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
                          Sci-fi Sounds  https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds
  rubberduck: 80 CC0 creature SFX        https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-creature-sfx
              80 CC0 creature SFX 2      https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-creture-sfx-2
  Ogrebane: Monster Sound Effects Pack   https://opengameart.org/content/monster-sound-effects-pack
  Swishes Sound Pack                     https://opengameart.org/content/swishes-sound-pack
Each sound layers several recordings, pitched down to the scale of 80 m giants, filtered and normalised.
To rebuild: unzip the audio files of every pack, flat, into tools/raw/ and run  python tools/design_sfx.py  (needs ffmpeg).
