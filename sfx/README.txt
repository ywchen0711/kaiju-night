Kaiju Night sound effects

Built by tools/design_sfx.py from real recordings in two packs by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), both CC0 (public domain):
  Impact Sounds   https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
  Sci-fi Sounds   https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds
Each sound layers several recordings, pitched down to the scale of 80 m giants, filtered and normalised.
To rebuild: unzip the .ogg files of both packs into tools/kenney_raw/ and run  python tools/design_sfx.py  (needs ffmpeg).
